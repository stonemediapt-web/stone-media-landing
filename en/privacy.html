Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Stone Media. For any question about your data, contact geral@stonemedia.pt.

2. What data we collect

  • Contact form: name, email, company or brand (optional), service of interest and the message you write.
  • Email: your address and the content of what you send us.
  • Analytics: if you accept cookies, usage data collected by Google Analytics, such as pages viewed, device and approximate location.
  • Technical data: IP address and browser information, handled by the services that host and deliver the site.

3. What we use the data for

PurposeLegal basis

We do not sell your data, do not send marketing without your consent and do not make automated decisions that affect you.

4. How long we keep the data

Contact requests that do not lead to a contract are kept for up to 24 months after the last contact. Client data is kept for the duration of the business relationship and then for the periods required by law, including tax law. Analytics cookies last as stated in section 7.

5. Who we share the data with

We use service providers that process data on our behalf or that the site needs in order to work:

  • Vercel: site hosting.
  • Supabase: database for contact requests and site content, and therefore receives your IP address when you open the home page.
  • EmailJS: sending the notification for each contact request.
  • ImprovMX and Google (Gmail): email forwarding and receipt.
  • Google Analytics: analytics, only with your consent.

Meeting scheduling (Calendly), Instagram and Facebook are external sites with their own privacy policies, which only apply if you open them through our links.

6. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. In those cases, the transfer must rely on safeguards set out in the GDPR, such as European Commission adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.

8. Your rights

You can request access to, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability of your data, object to processing based on legitimate interest, and withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing already carried out. Just write to geral@stonemedia.pt.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority, CNPD (cnpd.pt).

9. Information for consumers

You can file a complaint through the Electronic Complaints Book. For consumer disputes, you can turn to an alternative dispute resolution body. The list is available on the Portuguese Consumer Portal.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated when the site or the services it uses change. The date at the top shows the version in force.
